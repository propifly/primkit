# Primkit Decision Ledger

Primary runtime artifact: `.canon/brand/brand-source-map/decision-ledger.md`.

This ledger is the human seed view of material brand decisions identified by the current archaeology-mode source map. It records evidence and open production blockers without upgrading current site usage into final brand authority.

## Classification Coverage

This ledger represents only classifications identified in the supplied sources and upstream source-map artifacts.

| Classification | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| `Confirmed` | Represented. Product facts and owner decisions are captured from `SOURCE:brief-product-summary`, `SOURCE:owner-answers-first-pass`, and `SOURCE:source-map-instructions-this-pass`. |
| `De facto` | Represented. Current public site language, proof mode, robot asset usage, social preview usage, and active provisional tokens are observed across operating material. These rows are not final approval. |
| `Missing` | Represented. Production-ready voice, visual identity, source-grade assets, ownership, typography, accessibility, application rules, and related governance decisions are absent from the supplied sources. |
| `Rejected` | Represented. Owner-provided anti-references, forbidden labels, and rejected directions are recorded as constraints. |
| `Inferred` | Not identified in the supplied sources. No model-only conclusions are recorded as material decisions in this ledger. |
| `Proposed` | Not identified in the supplied sources. Source-silent needs are recorded as `Missing`, not invented proposals. |
| `Conflicting` | Not identified in the supplied sources. `.canon/brand/brand-source-map/conflicts.md` records no actual conflicting brand decisions. |

Source role, source quality, provenance, evidence, confidence, and approval state remain separate from classification. Source roles and source quality are recorded in `.canon/brand/brand-source-map/source-inventory.md` and `.canon/brand/brand-source-map/source-quality.md`; provenance limits are recorded in `.canon/brand/brand-source-map/asset-inventory.md` and `.canon/brand/brand-source-map/source-quality.md#blocked-evidence`.

| Decision group | Approval state |
| --- | --- |
| `Confirmed` rows | Directly supported by authoritative brief or owner-answer material as stated. |
| `De facto` rows | Observed current operating usage only; owner must confirm or consciously change it downstream. |
| `Rejected` rows | Explicitly ruled out by owner-supplied source material. |
| `Missing` rows | Open decisions with no production approval in the supplied sources. |

## Production Blocker Flags

`Yes` means the decision blocks production-grade brand guidance, production asset packaging, or approved application rules. It does not block completion of the source-map task.

`No` means the decision is still important for traceability, direction generation, or downstream confirmation, but the row does not by itself block this source-map deliverable.
